## The drawer's countdown is frozen

The document's clock ticks — hourly for a fortnight, per second for the last minutes. The drawer's
report row calls the same function inside a render nothing re-triggers, so its number is whatever it
was when the band last drew. Same instant, same function, one surface not redrawing.

Nothing needs aligning and there is no second clock: the deadline is `opened + retain`, and the
registry already joins the open stamp onto every row.

## The clock's words say "3h 60m"

Read off this document's own leaf while presenting it. `spanWords` rounds the leftover minutes and
never carries into the hour, so four hours reads as three hours and sixty minutes. One line, and it
is the kind of thing only showing somebody finds.

## Navigation is not reachable from the sequence queue

The cue vocabulary is six verbs — say, highlight, point, press, call, home. Five resolve a selector
and draw. **`call` is the one designed to reach a command and it is a stub**, so the whole command
family is unreachable from a cue: a walk can only point at what is already on screen, and putting
the subject there happens out of band. Replay the sequence tomorrow and it narrates whatever
document happens to be open.

Navigation becomes the first beat of a walk, played through the queue like every other beat — and
the rule goes into the walkthrough skill, so no future walk is built the un-replayable way.

## Reports are kept too long by default

Most reports die soon — this one is four hours. The defaults were written as though every report
needed a fortnight to be useful, and that is backwards: a report that matters gets diverted, and
everything else should be gone before it becomes furniture.
